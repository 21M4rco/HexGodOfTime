HexGodOfStories (the God of Time mod) makes a player Loki, the god of stories. You can deceive, conjure, command minds, bend time and open the floor onto other worlds. Your powers grow over six disciplines and end in **Glorious Purpose**, a full transformation that remakes every spell. This release covers everything the mod does, as it stands at this build.

**Download:** `hexgodofstories-0.7.0.jar` below. Put it in the `mods` folder of the server and of every client. The `-sources` jar holds the source code and isn't needed to play.

## Getting powers

Nobody has powers until an operator grants them. Until then the mod shows no HUD, opens no screen, answers no key and records no progress.

- `/hgos unlock <player> on` grants the powers.
- `/hgos unlock <player> off` takes them back, along with anything the player has standing.

## Controls

All of these can be rebound in Minecraft's Controls menu.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **K** | The mastery archive: your disciplines, every ability (what it does, what unlocks it), and where to bind it |
| **Z X C V B N M** | Seven bind slots. **Tap** one to cast what's bound to it. **Hold** it for that spell's second move, or for spells that are held |
| **R** | Cast the last spell again, tapped or held the same way |
| **G** | Warping's destinations (inside a Warping realm, the way out), whatever spell is chosen. With The Deceiver in hand, G is Complete Evisceration instead |
| **H** | Put on or take off Glorious Purpose |
| **J** | Flight, where you're allowed it (Cosmic Flight in the mantle, and in your sanctum) |
| **Y** | Warping's reach: pull creatures out of the realm chosen with G |
| **U** | Utility: let go of what you hold, take down a false wall, leave a realm, resume your stopped time |
| **I** | Stillness: stop time (transformed only); press again to resume |
| **O** | Personal Rewind |
| **Left Alt** | Time Branch Unleashing: tap for the charged fist, hold for the full torrent (transformed only) |
| **Mouse wheel** while holding something with telekinesis | Push it away or pull it closer |
| **Attack / Use** with a conjured weapon | The weapon's cuts, throw, guard, or beam (see each weapon) |

The slots fill themselves as abilities unlock. To place one yourself, open the archive (K), click a slot (each is shown under its key) and then click the ability. The slots are drawn as a strip over the ability panel, with each spell's recovery shown over it and the chosen one lit. The time controls (Stillness, Rewind, Time Branch) have their own permanent keys and are never put in a slot.

## How powers grow

- **Six disciplines:** Mischief, Sorcery, Conjuration, Enchantment, Temporal Mastery and Glorious Purpose. Each runs from 0 to 1,000 mastery.
- **Training:** casting a spell successfully trains its discipline, at a limited rate.
- **Unlocking abilities:** each ability opens at a mastery level in its discipline (listed below).
- **Temporal Mastery** opens once you have **600 combined mastery** across Mischief, Sorcery, Conjuration and Enchantment.
- **Glorious Purpose** opens at **800 Temporal Mastery**. From then on, Temporal training also trains Glorious Purpose.
- **Temporal Energy:** you have 1,000, and some spells spend it. It refills at 2 a second, except while you wear the mantle or hold time stopped.
- Recovery times below are in seconds.

## Conjuration

You can carry only one conjured weapon at a time: forming one dissolves any other. Conjured weapons never enter your inventory, dissolve if dropped, and can't be used by anyone else.

|---|---|---|---|

| **The Deceiver** | 160 | 2.75 | A long, slender sword. **Hold:** the Master Cuts. **G:** Complete Evisceration |
| **Scepter** | 380 | 3.5 | The Chitauri Scepter: bolts and piercing beams. **Hold:** dismiss it |

### The dagger

- **Attack** cuts with four different strikes. **Use** throws it. A thrown dagger flies point-first, sticks in what it hits (riding the limb it struck) and opens a bleeding wound before it dissolves. Tap the key again with the dagger in hand to put it away.
- **Flurry (hold the key** within reach of a body you're looking at). The body is held stunned while you cut it forehand, backhand, down from high on the right and rising back, then push-kick it away. It bleeds for a second after, four hearts in all. You keep walking and turning freely, and the combo ends if you leave its reach or look away. If you aren't holding a dagger, one forms for the combo and is gone after. Recovery: 6 seconds.

### The Deceiver

- **Attacks:** four heavier, longer cuts that land with crit sparks, a deeper sound, a shove and a kick of your own view.
- **Running cuts (attack while sprinting).** Seven one-handed cuts: flat across either way, down from either shoulder, rising from either hip and up the middle. Each starts where the last one ended, so the blade sweeps back and forth, and you keep running. Out of the rising cut comes the **slam**: a lunge and a two-handed cut straight down that drives the body into the ground (nine damage in a wide reach), with the ground cracking under it.
- **Guard (hold use).** An easy stance with the blade low at your side. A shot from the front is slapped back the way it came and becomes yours. A blow from the front is parried in a burst of sparks, and its striker (creature or player) is stunned where it stands for half a second. Each costs a little energy.
- **Master Cuts (hold the key** within reach of a body you're looking at), after the German longsword masters: the wrath cut down from the shoulder, the thwart cut across at the head, a thrust, and a rising cut that lifts the body off its feet. Five and a half hearts and a second's bleeding. Recovery: 8 seconds.
- **At rest.** Stand still with it for a couple of seconds and you lean on one leg, hand on hip, its point on the ground.
- **Complete Evisceration (G with The Deceiver in hand).** You dash up to nine blocks at the body you're looking at and drive the blade through it, so the point comes out of its back, with blood bursting from both wounds.
  - **If it survives**, it's held on the blade a moment, five hearts down and bleeding, then torn off it.
  - **If the blow would kill it**, it isn't killed yet. The blade is torn out, you step back, and the sword comes down from high on the left through to the right hip, **cutting the body in two**. This works on any body: every mob of any size, players, and other mods' creatures.
  - **The cut.** The cut faces are raw and blood red (seared and smoking if the blade is burning). The halves are thrown apart and fall limp like rag dolls, pouring blood.
  - **A creature** drops its loot at once, and its halves lie for 45 seconds before sinking away. A boss keeps its own death but is still seen as its halves.
  - **A player** lies as two halves for five seconds. They see it from their tumbling upper half and can only look around, and nothing can hurt them meanwhile. Then they die, credited to whoever cut them.
  - Nothing reached means a thrust into the air. A shield turns it aside. Recovery: 10 seconds (3 on a miss).
- **Burning (in Glorious Purpose).** Drawing The Deceiver lights it by hand. You run a palm up the flat of the blade, the fire chases it to the point, and you shake the sword out in a figure of eight.
  - It burns for as long as you hold it, and every blow sets its target alight.
  - Its use key no longer guards. Instead it pours a **jet of fire** from the point to wherever you look, up to sixteen blocks: a white-hot core in a widening column of flame, with a churning fireball where it strikes. It deals at most forty hearts to any one body per pour and draws Temporal Energy while it pours.
  - Whatever it kills comes apart.

### The Scepter

- **Tap** right click for a bolt, as fast as you like (up to five a second). A tap during recovery is queued and fires the moment it can.
- **Hold** for at least a second to fill the stone, then release a beam out to 100 blocks that burns through up to six bodies and detonates whatever it reaches last. Held the full ten seconds, it fires itself at full power.
- **Holes.** Every body a beam passes through keeps a cauterised **hole** you can see the world through, white-hot at the rim. The hole bleeds and closes over 7–11 seconds. Charged beams also stun and set on fire.
- **Last Moments.** A body a less-than-full beam would kill is left standing on its last breath for 3–4.5 seconds, bleeding hard, then dies the ordinary way. A full stone unmakes what it kills.
- **Melee:** a hit with it does two hearts and a second's bleeding.
- **Presentation:** a solid, metal-shaded staff with a glowing stone, its own first-person poses, and recorded laser sounds.

### Crown of Barrels

- **Hold** the key: both arms rise and fifteen machine guns form one by one in an arch over you, then fire ragged bursts wherever you look for as long as you hold.
- **The rounds** barely sting, but each one stuns for a second and every next one starts that second again. They pass through up to four bodies but never through blocks, and leave tiny bleeding holes. A shield raised toward you takes them.
- **Let go** and the guns come apart.
- **Hold for thirteen seconds** and you throw your arms down. Two missiles wander slowly to where you aimed and strike for forty hearts each, which no shield stops. They blow a crater that knits itself shut again afterwards, while a shockwave rolls the ground forty blocks across. Nothing is dropped or lost.
- **Gotcha! (tap while looking at a body).** A single gun forms silently behind it, turns onto its head and fires. It never misses unless a block is in the way: ten hearts, a bigger hole and five seconds of bleeding. No energy, with its own 7-second recovery.
- The guns, rockets and sounds are TACZ's work, credited in `ARSENAL_CREDITS.md`. TACZ isn't needed.

## Warping

- **Opening a pool.** Hold the cast key on solid ground and a black pool spreads across it, up to 16 blocks across, for as long as you hold. **G** picks where it leads. Release to open it, then sink through.
- **The pool.** It's liquid, not a hole: it lies over steps and slabs without breaking a block, shows the other world through it, and takes you like oil. It grips gently at the surface and harder the deeper you go, and draws you toward the middle. Hammering jump fights your way back out. Nothing suffocates in it.
- **Arriving.** Every arrival rises slowly out of a black puddle on the far side.
- **Return.** Inside any realm, **G** chooses where you'll come out (a named player, the nearest player, the Nether, your bed, or where you came from), and the cast key or **U** takes you there.
- **Reaching in (Y).** Instead of going to the realm you've chosen, you pull its creatures out. The ground cracks open where you look, and up to ten of the realm's creatures climb out, carried across intact.
- **No realm is a life sentence.** Anything a pool drops into a realm is let out after two minutes at most, rising where it was taken. Players hear a countdown. Keepers (anyone with Warping) are never held.
- **Who it takes.** Other Warping keepers and creative players can't be trapped. The Void Sea's god can never be brought out.

## The World Tree sanctum

- **The island.** Your own floating island of about 150 blocks, with a great branching tree, root groves, luminous flowers and a carved **throne** you can sit on (right-click; crouch to stand).
- **Inside it you can't be struck.** An attack is refused before it lands, and you reappear a few blocks away through the nebula.
- **The island fights back.** Anything that hunts or hurts you there draws a green falling star (about ten hearts) that never harms you or the island.
- **Way out.** As in any realm, G inside chooses where you leave to.

## Wounds and blood

- **Bleeding cripples.** Every cut, throw, beam and bite opens wounds that bleed. A bleeding body is slowed to a quarter of its speed, reels with nausea and can't jump, until two seconds after it stops.
- **Blood goes where the blade went.** Each cut throws blood the way the blade travelled; a thrust goes out through the back.
- **Blood pools** form where it lands. The puddles spread, merge as more blood runs in, shine while wet, dry to a deep red, follow the blocks they lie on and never flicker. Drops are a clean red and leave a mark stretched the way they flew.
- **No potion swirls.** None of the mod's stuns, dazes or bleeding show a potion's particles; the blood is all there is to see.

## Presentation

- **Whole-body moves.** Every blade move is the whole body, posed through Player Animator: weight shifts, steps and lunges, the head on the target, and the dagger flipping between grips.
- **The cloak** is a cloth simulation in world space. It trails while you run, lifts on a fall and swings on a sharp turn, and is never stretched across the world.
- **Its own look.** A mantle, horns, outfit, authored weapon models, a nebula particle family and a post-processing chain.
- **Stopped time.** Stopped bodies are drawn at one fixed instant, without shivering.
- **Long-running servers.** Timing is exact on servers that have run for weeks.

## Alongside HexKagunes

When HexKagunes is installed too:

- **No stuck bodies.** No body is ever left frozen or floating by a mix of stuns, holds, erasures and tendril grabs. Each hold learns the body's own gravity and AI, and the last to let go puts them back. A player who leaves while held is saved on their own feet.
- **One hold at a time.** The unseen hand and Gravity Grasp leave tendril-held bodies alone.
- **A shot is turned only once.** The Deceiver's guard, Mirror Ward and the sanctum answer a shot before the tendrils' swat does.
- **Halved players are harmless.** A player cut in two harms nothing for the five seconds they lie there, and a halved or erased body has no tendrils drawn.
- **Bleeding.** HexKagunes' bleeding is drawn with this mod's blood.
- **The link.** `api.KaguneLink` tells HexKagunes 1.0.7 when its player is frozen, stunned, halved or erased, and which bodies its tendrils must leave alone.
